Qisqacha: Alifbo siz yozgan matnni hech qayerga yubormaydi. Klaviatura internetga ulanmaydi va «To‘liq ruxsat» so‘ramaydi. Hisob ochish, reklama va kuzatish yo‘q.

1. Klaviatura

  • Klaviatura «To‘liq ruxsat» (Full Access) so‘ramaydi, shuning uchun iOS unga internet va boshqa ilovalar ma’lumotiga kirishni bermaydi.
  • Siz yozgan matn faqat telefonda qayta ishlanadi va hech qayerga yuborilmaydi.
  • So‘z takliflari uchun klaviatura siz ko‘p yozgan so‘zlarni faqat telefoningizda eslab qoladi. Ularni klaviatura sozlamalaridagi «O‘rgangan so‘zlarni o‘chirish» tugmasi bilan o‘chirishingiz mumkin.

2. Ilova va «Ulashish» orqali o‘girish

  • Matn va fayllarni (Word, PDF, TXT) o‘girish to‘liq telefonda bajariladi. Fayllar va matnlar serverga yuklanmaydi.
  • Ilovada reklama, analitika va kuzatuv vositalari yo‘q.

3. Xaridlar (Alifbo Pro)

Alifbo Pro xaridi Apple App Store orqali amalga oshiriladi. To‘lov ma’lumotlaringizni biz ko‘rmaymiz. Xaridni tekshirish va tiklash uchun ilova RevenueCat xizmatidan foydalanadi. RevenueCat’ga quyidagilar yuboriladi:

  • tasodifiy, anonim foydalanuvchi identifikatori (ismingiz, telefon raqamingiz yoki emailingiz emas);
  • xarid tarixi (qaysi mahsulot, qachon sotib olingani) va App Store tranzaksiya ma’lumotlari;
  • ilova versiyasi, qurilma modeli va iOS versiyasi kabi texnik ma’lumotlar.

Bu ma’lumotlar faqat xaridni tasdiqlash va tiklash uchun ishlatiladi, reklama yoki kuzatish uchun emas.

4. Bolalar

Ilova barcha yoshdagilar uchun mo‘ljallangan va bolalardan shaxsiy ma’lumot to‘plamaydi.

5. O‘zgarishlar

Privacy Policy (English)

Effective: November 12, 2026

Short version: Alifbo never sends what you type anywhere. The keyboard does not request Full Access and has no network access. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Keyboard

The keyboard extension does not request Full Access, so iOS gives it no network access. Text is processed on the device only. Words you type often are remembered on the device for suggestions and can be erased in the keyboard settings.

App and share action

Text and file (Word, PDF, TXT) conversion happens entirely on the device. Nothing is uploaded. There are no ads, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Purchases

Alifbo Pro is sold through the App Store; we never see your payment details. To verify and restore purchases the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random anonymous user ID, your purchase history and App Store transaction data, and basic technical data (app version, device model, iOS version). This data is used only to provide and restore purchases, never for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from children.
